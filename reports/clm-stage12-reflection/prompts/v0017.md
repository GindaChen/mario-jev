You control Mario in Super Mario Bros. World 1-2. Reach the native flag event alive. Select an offered action from the current observed geometry and motion. Each choice lasts at most four native frames, sometimes less on landing.

First check whether moving right during the next choice is safe. If continuous floor supports the route and no enemy, pit, or solid ground-level obstacle can intersect Mario in the next few frames, select right_run. This rule applies when grounded, rising, falling, or just entering the level. In particular, at opening x=40, the floor continues right, the raised tiles have open space underneath, and the first Goomba is distant: select right_run. If horizontal velocity is zero and no immediate hazard blocks the route, Mario needs Right and B to begin making progress. Jump alone supplies no rightward acceleration. Do not choose jump merely to remain airborne, wait for a distant enemy, or hold an earlier A press. Recheck the geometry after moving.

For a same-height enemy, compare its horizontal_gap_px with Mario's speed and the room needed to rise. At the opening Goomba pair, move right until the nearer gap is about 90–110 px while grounded, then select right_run_jump while support and overhead clearance remain. If that takeoff window has passed, jump right immediately while there is still clearance; a gap near 35 px may already be too late. For other enemies, start a moving jump before contact becomes imminent. While airborne over an enemy, steer right toward a safe landing; A cannot start another jump in midair. Resume rightward travel after landing when safe.

An overhead tile with empty space beneath it is passable underneath, not a ground-level wall. A distant wall does not cancel a nearer enemy threat. If grounded with A already held and another jump selected, the fixed controller executes one release frame first, so the selected and executed actions can differ.

Jump right while support remains when approaching a floor gap with a visible landing bank. Pressing A after walking off cannot rescue a fall. If standing on a raised surface with continuous lower floor beyond its edge, run off and land below before another jump unless a visible hazard intersects the drop. If blocked, reconsider the geometry and motion.
